AI video experiment — 001

One video.Any language.Same face.

An experimental AI video-to-video pipeline that translates speech, generates a new voice and synchronizes the speaker’s lips to the translated dialogue.

Read the case study

  1. 01 — Source

    The original video.

    One presenter, one take, in English. Meera is a virtual anchor — she has never set foot in a studio.

  2. 02 — Audio

    Isolate the voice.

    The soundtrack becomes a waveform the models can reason about.

  3. 03 — Transcript

    Every word, timestamped.

    Speech recognition catches every word — 53 of them, each pinned to the millisecond.

  4. 04 — Language

    Detect the language.

    English, with 99.7% confidence. Now pick a new one.

  5. 05 — Translation

    Rewrite it in Hindi.

    Sentence by sentence, for meaning rather than word for word. The story keeps its shape.

  6. 06 — Voice

    Give her a Hindi voice.

    A synthetic voice reads the translation — no booth, no voice actor, no second session.

  7. 07 — Lip sync

    Move the lips to match.

    Her face is animated to the new audio, frame by frame, so every syllable lands on the lips.

  8. 08 — Output

    Same face. New language.

    No camera, no crew, no studio. Change the script and Meera presents it in another language.

  9. 09 — Pipeline

    Five models. One timeline.

    Video → speech → translation → voice → lips → video. Time stays aligned from the first word to the last frame.

      (07) How I build
      How I build: idea, prototype, experiment, break, iterate, ship
      01 / 06
      1. 01Idea
      2. 02Prototype
      3. 03Experiment
      4. 04Break
      5. 05Iterate
      6. 06Ship
      1. Ideas are cheap.
        I write them down, then wait to see which ones keep coming back.
      2. So I build one the same day.
        Ugly, fast, real. A prototype is a question you can hold.
      3. Experiments reveal what matters.
        Measure it, show it to someone, watch where they hesitate.
      4. Then I try to break it.
        Edge cases, bad input, slow networks, a model having a bad day.
      5. Code turns experiments into products.
        Keep what survived. Delete what didn’t. Loop.
      6. Shipping makes them real.
        App Store, crates.io, Chrome Web Store, a live newsroom. Done is a feature.
